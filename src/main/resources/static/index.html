HOW IT WORKS

Finding coffer value

Death's Coffer credits an eligible item at 105% of its official GE guide value. This site compares that credit against the recent real-time GE price observed by the OSRS Wiki/RuneLite price system.

Coffer value = Official GE × 1.05
Saving = Coffer value − Live buy

Practical score is only a ranking aid. It reduces the influence of huge-looking discounts when the item has very little trading volume or an awkward buy limit.

Low-confidence opportunities remain visible because they can still be useful, but they deserve extra checking before you spend GP.
